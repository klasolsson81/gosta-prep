Welcome to the GÖSTA fair of 2026!

GÖSTA gives you as a student a unique opportunity to meet companies in the IT industry and make valuable contacts for the future. Get a chance to explore your area of interest within IT and which companies you see as potential employers. We guarantee a fantastic day with many inspiring and rewarding conversations!

During the day you will also have the opportunity to attend two lectures where different interesting topics will be addressed.

When: Thursday, February 19, 10:00 – 15:00

Where: Lindholmen Conference center, Lindholmspiren 5, 417 56 Göteborg

We look forward to meeting you!

Tips!

Come prepared

Think about what interests you, what you want to get out of the fair, and which companies you want to talk to. Check in the fair catalog before you visit the fair, where you can read more about this year’s exhibitors. Feel free to write down the questions you want to ask the companies!

Help yourself

The companies are at the fair to meet the students, so do not be afraid to go ahead. Bring the questions you wrote down, it gives the impression that you are interested and prepared.

Get in touch

If there was a company that caught a little extra interest, then take a business card or ask for contact details. Write to them after the fair and thank them for the conversation. By following up on your conversation, you give a stronger and long-lasting impression.

Schedule

08.30 Breakfast lecture with Axel Arigato

This time, we’re joined by Axel Arigato who will give an lecture on how digitalization and AI can amplify how people build, think, and experience, illustrated through real examples from Axel Arigato

The breakfast lecture is held in Torg Grön, Patricia building at Forskningsgången 6 between 08.30-09.30. The lecture will be held in English.

10.00 THE FAIR OPENS

We want to welcome all of you students to this year’s GÖSTA fair!

Here you have the opportunity to talk to several companies in the industry, gain more insight into what it is like in the world of work and possibly meet your new employers.

The first 200 people who visit the fair will also get a lottery ticket for Göstas lottery with very lovely prizes!

12.00 Lunch lecture with Deloitte

Discover Cybersecurity with Deloitte – Snapshots from Our Projects

Welcome to a Lunch & Learn where our colleagues will briefly guide you through how we at Deloitte are leading the way in Swedish information security.

Focus areas:

  • The areas we operate in
  •  What we deliver
  • Snapshots from our projects
  •  Q&A session

We are looking for future colleagues who want to grow, develop, and contribute from day one.

The lunch lecture will be held in Torg Grön in the Patricia building at Forskningsgången 6 from 12:00-13:00. Tickets is sold on Orbi. Please note, the lecture will be conducted in Swedish.

15.00 THE FAIR CLOSES

The fair closes for this year, many thanks to everyone who came and visited the GÖSTA Fair 2026! We hope you enjoyed the day and met some companies that caught your interest.

Help us to get better, feel free to respond to our evaluation of this year’s fair!
